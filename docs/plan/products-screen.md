# The Products screen

Moved out of PLAN.md on 2026-10-28, unchanged, to keep PLAN.md short enough to read. PLAN.md is still the overview and the source of truth, and this file is part of it. "The Syncfusion section" means [syncfusion.md](syncfusion.md); a section named without a file (Domain Model, Application Architecture, Reactive Costing Cascade) is [PLAN.md](../../PLAN.md)'s.

- **One Products screen (`ProductsView`/`ProductsViewModel`), rebuilt from the user's Cream Loaf / Chocolate Cake mockups (2026-09-14): the Raw Materials shape, with one Save.** A product is the mockups' "recipe". The app shell stays as it is, so the mockups' sidebar, breadcrumb and Back button were left out, as were the product photo, sub-assembly descriptions (sections show the role), "by Admin" (the app has no users) and the usage example's product picker.
  - **Product categories** (2026-09-17, at the user's request; the Domain Model has the entity):
    - **The header's Category picker works as Raw Materials' does**: a name typed into it that isn't a category brings up `TextPromptWindow` to add it (`ProductsViewModel.CommitCategoryTextAsync`, the same `ICategoryPickerScreen` flow; the "< Add New .. >" row it had until 2026-09-26 went). It lists `Categories` directly, since there's no type to filter by. Unlike Raw Materials, adding one reloads only the categories, not the whole screen. Verified in the app: Tab out of "Zz Cakes" opened the prompt, and Escape left the product with no category. Deleting the picker's text clears the category, and Save then saves none: verified in the app, since a category is optional here.
    - **The list is a tree of folders** (2026-09-26, at the user's request: "convert the Products List into a TreeView with folders containing products", with "two top level folders: Production and Research and Development" and a way to drag a product to a folder). It's an `SfTreeView` in `ProductListCard` over `ProductsViewModel.ProductTree` (`ViewModels/ProductTree.cs`); the Syncfusion section has what the control took.
      - **Two stage folders**, Production then Research and Development (`Product.Stage`, see the Domain Model). Each holds a folder for every product category, by name, then Uncategorized, and each of those its products, by name. The user chose categories as the sub-folders, over folders of their own making, and chose Research and Development for a new product; since 2026-10-07 New's stage is a setting, Production to start with (`CostingDefaults.NewProductStage`).
      - **Every category has a folder in each stage**, so an empty one can be dropped on. A search shows only the folders holding a match, opened, and they stay open once it's cleared. The stages start open and the category folders closed; a folder stays as it was left (`ProductFolderNode.IsExpanded`). **An open folder shows an open-folder icon** (2026-09-26, at the user's request): Segoe Fluent Icons' FolderOpen (U+E838) in place of Folder (U+E8B7), following `IsExpanded`, which the tree writes as a folder is clicked open or shut. It shows only while the folder has rows under it, so an empty folder left open still looks closed (`ProductFolderNode.Glyph`).
      - **Dragging a product onto a folder moves it at once, with no Save** (`MoveProductsCommand`, `IProductRepository.MoveAsync`). Onto a category folder or Uncategorized sets its stage and category, onto a stage's folder its stage alone, and onto another product that product's folder (`ProductTree.MoveTo`). Folders can't be dragged, and a drop that would leave the product where it is is refused. The folder it lands in opens, to show it arriving. **Several products picked with Ctrl or Shift move together** (2026-09-26; `ProductTree.MovesTo`, those already there left out), in one save.
      - **A right-click opens a menu for the row, or for the list** (2026-09-26, at the user's request: "suggest improvement and functionality to the Product TreeView like right clicking a product or right clicking a folder", then "implement all your suggestions"). `ProductsViewModel.TreeMenuFor` describes each menu as `TreeMenuItem`s (header, command and parameter, key, glyph, submenu), so the tests check what each offers and run it as a click would; `ProductListCard` only turns them into `MenuItem`s. A row right-clicked outside the selection is picked alone first, as in File Explorer, so the product it opens on is the one showing; one inside it keeps the rest.
        - **A product**: Promote to Production or Send Back to Research and Development (keeping its category), **Move to ▸** a stage ▸ any category or Uncategorized (its own folder greyed), Rename… (F2), Clone…, Create Variant…, and Delete (Del). **Several products**: only the moves, counted ("Promote 2 Products to Production", "Move 3 Products to"), each moving just the products it would change.
        - **A stage's folder**: New Product Here, New Category…, Expand All, Collapse All. **A category's folder**: New Product Here, Promote All to Production (Research and Development only, when it shows products; with a search on, the matches), New Category…, Rename Category… (F2), Delete Category (Del). **Uncategorized**: New Product Here, and Promote All in Research and Development. **The empty space**: New Product, New Category…, Expand All, Collapse All.
        - **F2 and Del do what the one row picked's menu shows beside them** (`TreeShortcut`), and nothing with several picked, where a key could take the wrong one; Enter opens or closes a folder. The menu key or Shift+F10 opens the row's menu under it.
      - **Each move says what it did, with Undo, on a notice bar under the tree** (`MoveNotice`, `UndoMoveCommand`): "Moved Pandesal to Production › Breads", "Moved 3 products to Production › Breads", "Moved 3 products to Production" (a Promote from several folders), or "Moved 3 products". Undo moves each back where it was (another `MoveAsync`), and is refused with "The category to move it to no longer exists." when the category it came from was deleted since (`MoveAsync` checks the categories rather than let the foreign key's refusal read as a duplicate name). The bar is Mauve, the neutral notice's colour, as wide as the tree so it wraps rather than widening the card, and it hides itself 8 seconds on unless the mouse is over it, or with its close button. The Products screen has no success toast, so this is its only notice.
      - **New Product Here** (`NewProductInCommand`) starts a new product as New does, with the Category picker on the folder's category and the folder's stage kept for Save (`NewProductStage`, which Revert keeps too, with the category); the cursor goes to Name. New from the header starts one with no category in Settings' stage for a new product (`_defaultNewProductStage`, Production unless Settings says otherwise; 2026-10-07). **While the product isn't saved, a tag beside the header's title says where Save will file it**: "Goes in Production" or "Goes in Research and Development" (`NewProductTag`), styled as the "Moves to Sub-Assemblies on Save" badge (`LinkedBadgeStyle`, 11px Warning text), docked in a left-aligned `DockPanel` after the title so a long name trims with the tag still showing. The blank form the screen opens on shows Settings' stage. A new product started by New (`_newProductInDefaultStage`) follows that setting when a load brings a changed one (`RefreshCostingDefaults`), typed into or not, since its stage was never picked; Revert starts it in the setting's stage again. One started by New Product Here keeps its folder's. The user chose this (2026-09-26), asked how New Product Here on a Production folder should work, over filing every new product in Research and Development (the item offered only there), creating the product at once with a name prompt, or a Stage field on the form.
      - **Rename… saves the name at once** (`RenameProductCommand`, `IProductRepository.RenameAsync`, which renames the product's own sub-assemblies with it, as a Save's rename does). **On the product showing with unsaved edits, only the name (or, for a move, the stage and category) is saved, and the rest stays unsaved**: the user's choice (2026-09-26) over asking each time, saving everything first, making Rename an edit of the Name box for Save to write, or blocking Rename and the moves while there are unsaved edits. On the product the form shows, it's handled as a move's category is: the name box takes the new name, the Clone tab's name follows while it's still "… Copy", and every undo step takes it (`RewriteAll`), so whatever else is typed stays to be saved. Clone… and Create Variant… ask for the new product's name (starting from "… Copy") and then do what the Clone tab's buttons do, which is to copy the product as saved and show the new one.
      - **New Category…, Rename Category… and Delete Category** do in the list what Settings' Product Categories tab does: a category is a folder in both stages, which the prompts say. A new one's folder is picked in the stage it was asked in (a name already listed picks that one's folder instead); a rename keeps the Category picker on it; Delete Category asks first, naming how many products it leaves uncategorized, then reloads, and rewrites the form's undo steps so none puts back a category that no longer exists. The prompts are `PromptForName`, a `TextPromptWindow` per `TextPromptRequest`.
      - **Each folder shows its product count**, muted after its name ("Breads (12)"; a stage's, all of its own); with a search on, its matches (`ProductFolderNode.ProductCount`).
      - **The Deleted folder** (2026-10-06, see Deleted products in the Domain Model): a third top-level folder after the stages (`ProductTree.DeletedFolder`, in `Roots`; `Stages` is the two stages), bold like them (`ProductTreeNode.IsTopLevel`), with the Delete glyph (the bin) open or closed. It's closed to start with, its key is "Deleted", and Expand All and Collapse All on the list take it along. It holds the deleted products straight by name, whatever their stage and category (`ProductsViewModel.DeletedProducts`, filtered by the search, which opens it on a match). A product deleted or restored keeps its leaf, which moves between the folders. **A deleted row is picked as a folder is**: the form stays on what it showed, the row stays picked, and `SelectedTreeProducts` leaves it out. Its menu is **Restore** (puts it back and opens it) and **Delete Permanently** (Del, asks first, "This can't be undone."). The folder itself has no menu. It can't be dragged, and nothing is dropped on the folder or on a deleted row (`ProductTree.MoveTo`). The header's Delete and the list's Delete ask "Delete 'Mamon'? It moves to the Deleted folder at the bottom of the list, where it can be restored as it is."
      - **The folders left open open again next time** (`IViewStateStore`, `JsonViewStateStore` at `%LocalAppData%\TinapayanRMS\view-state.json`, key `Products.OpenFolders`). Each folder has a key that lasts between runs (`ProductFolderNode.Key`: "Production", "Production/12", "ResearchAndDevelopment/Uncategorized"); `ProductTree` starts each folder open if its key was saved, and reports every opening and closing (`ExpansionChanged`), which the screen writes half a second after the last change, off the UI thread, and once more as it's disposed (`FolderStateWriter`, shared with the Raw Materials and Sub-Assemblies trees since 2026-10-28). Nothing is written until a folder is actually opened or closed: `CompositionRootTests` disposes the real ViewModels, and would otherwise have written into the developer's own profile. Kept out of the database because it's one person's view, not the shop's data; a missing or broken file reads as nothing saved.
      - **On the product the form shows, the Category picker follows the move, and the move is written into every undo step** (`UndoHistory.RewriteAll`). Save stays as it was, and no undo brings the old category back for a Save to write. Anything else typed into the form stays, unsaved.
      - **Picking a product's row picks the product** (`SelectedTreeNode`). Clicking a folder opens or closes it and leaves the product showing. The row follows `SelectedProduct` however that changes: opened from Sub-Assemblies' Used By tiles, saved, cloned, or cleared by New.

  - **One Save writes the whole product.**
    - Any open form is applied first, then `ProductValidator` and `CostingParametersValidator` run.
    - The form is compared with what was loaded (the baseline: the product's recipe, its packaging lines and its costing). `IProductRepository.SaveAsync(product, recipe?, packagingLines?, costing?)` gets only what changed: the whole `RecipeDraft` (its own sub-assemblies with their lines, and its links, each with its piece size) or null; the packaging lines or null; the costing or null. (Until 2026-09-14 it took each unlinked role whose usage set differed.)
    - One context and one transaction, as for Raw Materials: the product (added or updated from a copy of its fields, stamping `UpdatedAt`), its recipe, the packaging lines, the costing, then the commit. Each step goes through `internal static` write methods (`ProductRepository.AddAsync`/`UpdateAsync`, `ProductSubAssemblyUsageRepository.SetRecipeAsync` — `SetUsagesForRoleAsync` until 2026-09-14 — `PackagingLineRepository.SetLinesAsync`, `CostingParametersRepository.SetAsync`), so every check (own and linked sub-assemblies, names, lines, piece sizes, duplicates, linked packaging, packaging type) runs, and a rejection anywhere rolls everything back — sub-assemblies included. The caller's entities are never attached, so a rolled-back save leaves no id on them. `ProductCloner` adds its product through the same static `AddAsync`.
    - Afterwards `LoadCommand` runs and a fresh copy of the product is selected (`ShowSavedProduct`, with the product and detail the Save read back inside its unit of work, since 2026-10-28), so the form and its baseline are what was saved. `LoadCommand`'s ten reads go at once on the thread pool (2026-09-28): each is mostly a round trip's wait, and one after another, each resuming on the UI thread, they took 130–250 ms against the dev database, against about 10 ms at once (measured outside the app).
    - **Since 2026-09-23, Save waits for its reload**: it keeps each new section on the Sub-Assemblies screen, and until the reload shows them linked the form still has them as new, so a second Save in that moment would keep each twice. `ReloadSavedProductAsync` cleared `AreDetailsReady`, which Save already waits on and the tabs' hit-testing follows, and the reload set it again. **Since 2026-09-28 the reload finishes inside Save** (the user reported a flicker on every Save): selecting the same product closes its form (`ResetDetails`), and the detail was read only after that, so the screen drew the form without its section until the read came back and opened it again. Then `ReloadSavedProductAsync` read the product and its detail first, at once on the thread pool (since 2026-10-28 the Save reads them back itself, and `ShowSavedProduct` takes them), and handed the detail to the selection's load (`_preloadedDetail`), which shows it without awaiting: the form closes and opens again in one pass. Nothing is left to arrive once Save returns, so it no longer clears `AreDetailsReady`. Delete's confirmation now says the product's sub-assemblies stay on the Sub-Assemblies screen, and nothing but a packaging link refuses it.
    - Linking, copying and Make a Copy of a sub-assembly are recipe edits, saved by Save. The packaging link and Clone stay immediate: Link and Unlink Packaging reload only the link and the packaging lines with their baseline, so unsaved edits elsewhere survive. Clone copies the product as saved — its own copy of every sub-assembly. Delete's confirmation says the product's own sub-assemblies go with it, and it's refused while another product links one of them or its packaging.
