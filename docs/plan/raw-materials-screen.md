Moved out of PLAN.md on 2026-10-28, unchanged, to keep PLAN.md short enough to read. PLAN.md is still the overview and the source of truth, and this file is part of it. "The Syncfusion section" means [syncfusion.md](syncfusion.md); a section named without a file (Domain Model, Application Architecture, Reactive Costing Cascade) is [PLAN.md](../../PLAN.md)'s.

- **One Raw Materials screen (`RawMaterialsView`/`RawMaterialsViewModel`), from the user's Ingredients mockup (2026-09-13): the list beside the selected material's cards.** Ingredients and Packaging Materials were two copies of this layout until the raw materials merge the same day; one screen now covers both, and a material's type decides which cards and fields apply.
  - **The list is a tree of folders** (2026-09-27, at the user's request: "Product List has been converted to a TreeView, we can do the same with the Raw Materials"). It's an `SfTreeView` in `MaterialListCard` over `RawMaterialsViewModel.RawMaterialTree` (`ViewModels/RawMaterialTree.cs`), 370px wide as Products' is, and it works as the Products tree does (see the Products screen's list, and the Syncfusion section's `SfTreeView` notes, which hold for both). The user settled three things: full parity with the Products tree, the material's type for the top-level folders, and Delete Category greyed on a folder that holds materials.
    - **Two type folders, Ingredients then Packaging**, each holding a folder for every category *of that type* (categories are per type), by name, and each of those its materials, by name. **There's no Uncategorized**: every material has a category. The types start open and the categories closed. Each folder shows its material count, and a search shows only the folders holding a match, opened, as on Products.
    - **Dragging materials onto a category folder, or onto a material in one, moves them there at once** (`MoveMaterialsCommand`, `IRawMaterialRepository.MoveAsync`). `MoveAsync` sets the category alone, for any number of materials in one save, stamps `UpdatedAt`, and refuses a category that no longer exists or is of the other type. **A material's type is fixed, so a drop into the other type is refused**, and so is a drop on a type's folder, which has no category to give. A category costs nothing, so nothing cascades.
      - The notice under the tree says what moved, with Undo, as on Products: "Moved Bread Flour to Ingredients › Dry Goods", "Moved 3 materials to Ingredients › Dry Goods", or "Moved 3 materials".
      - On the material the form shows, the Category picker follows and every undo step takes the new category (`History.RewriteAll`). Anything else typed stays unsaved.
    - **Right-click menus** (`RawMaterialsViewModel.TreeMenuFor`; `TreeMenuItem`s turned into `MenuItem`s by `Controls/TreeContextMenu`, which the Products list uses too):
      - **A material**: Move to ▸ its type's categories (its own greyed), Rename… (F2) and Delete (Del). Rename saves the name alone at once (`RenameAsync`), as on Products. **Several materials**: only Move N Materials to, and nothing at all when ingredients and packaging are picked together, since there's no category they could share.
      - **A type's folder**: New Ingredient Here or New Packaging Material Here, New Category…, Expand All, Collapse All. **A category's folder**: New … Here, New Category…, Rename Category… (F2), Delete Category (Del). **The empty space**: New Raw Material, New Ingredient Category…, New Packaging Category…, Expand All, Collapse All.
      - **New … Here** starts a new material of the folder's type, in its category for a category's folder. `ClearForm(type, categoryId)` sets both before the form settles, so it doesn't open with something to save. The cursor goes to Name.
      - **Delete Category is offered only for a category no material is in** (the user's choice, over moving its materials to another category first or leaving the item out). Otherwise it reads "Delete Category (holds 4)" and is greyed, and Del does nothing. It counts every material, not a search's matches: a material must have a category, and the foreign key refuses the delete anyway.
    - **The folders left open open again next time**, under `RawMaterials.OpenFolders` (keys "Ingredient", "Packaging", "Ingredient/12").
    - Shared with the Products tree since 2026-09-27: `TreeMenuItem` (was `ProductTreeMenuItem`), `Controls/TreeContextMenu` (the menus and `RowOf`, from `ProductListCard`) and `ViewModels/TreeRows` (`Arrange` and `RemoveMissing`, from `ProductTree`).
