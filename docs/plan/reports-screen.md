# The Reports screen

Part of [PLAN.md](../../PLAN.md). What each table shows is the screen state's (`ReportsState`), and how it's drawn is the Blazor page's (`Components/Pages/Reports.razor`).

## The screen

- **One card holding an `SfTab`**, a tab per table (Product Cost Summary, Raw Materials Price Summary, Cost Buffers, Cost Drivers and Margin Erosion), each a read-only `SfGrid` under a heading, an Export to Excel button and a caption. The tab strip is given no `Height` ([maui-app.md](maui-app.md), "How it's built").
- **A tab's table is built the first time its tab is shown** (`LoadOn="ContentLoad.Demand"`), then kept while the page is open, with its sort, filters and picks; its layout is put back then. The record screens' tabs are all built as they open (`Init`), for their forms; built that way here, the five tables were each drawn twice a visit, with the last visit's rows and then the new ones. A table shown for the first time takes about a quarter of a second.
- **Each table sorts by a click on a heading and filters from the funnel in it** (Excel-style filters, `GridFilterSettings Type="Excel"`, on its text columns). Its rows and heading are 24px; Product Cost Summary's headings wrap onto two lines (`AllowTextWrap`), each line 20px.
- **Each table's filters, sort and columns are as they were left**, for the Windows user: the grid's own state (`GetPersistDataAsync`) is kept in the view state after each sort, filter or column change (`GridLayouts`, under `reports-product-costs-grid` and its like) and put back as the grid is created.
- **A double-click on a row opens its product or material** on its own screen (`OnRecordDoubleClick`, through `OpenProductCommand` and `OpenRawMaterialCommand`, whose requests `ScreenLinks` turns into the record's address), the screen being left asking about its unsaved changes first.
- **Product Cost Summary's Columns** opens the grid's own column chooser (`ShowColumnChooser`): every column but Product can be hidden.
- **Export to Excel saves the table as it's shown**: the grid's own export (`ExportToExcelAsync(asMemoryStream: true)`), so its order, filters and only the columns showing come with it, saved where Windows' Save dialog says (`IFileDialogs`, `ExcelWorkbook`). A file open in Excel is an error notice; the workbook is written to memory first, so that file is left untouched.

## The tables

- **Product Cost Summary** (`ProductCostRow`): every product as saved, priced through `SavedProductReader`, so it agrees with Products and Print, one sellable unit each: Product, Stage, Category, Pieces, Ingredients Cost, Wastage Cost, Packaging Cost, Labor Cost, Overhead Cost, Admin Cost, Base Cost, Suggested Price (the Costing tab's Suggested Selling Price), Rounded Price (rounded up by Settings' price rounding), Current Price, Markup % (what the current price earns), Target Markup % (the Markup Metric), Short By (how far the markup is short of it, blank when it isn't) and No Piece Size (the sections with no weight per piece or yield, which cost the product nothing, by name: `ProductCostRow.NoPieceSize`, from `SavedProduct.SectionsWithoutPieceSize`). Current Price, Markup % and Short By are blank without a current price.
  - **Product, Stage and Category filter** from the funnel in their heading; the figures don't.
- **Raw Materials Price Summary** (`RawMaterialPriceSummaryRow`): every purchased material, by name: Raw Material, Type, Category, Unit, Price, Change % and Since. Price, Unit and Since are the latest price-history entry; Change % is its cost per base unit against the entry before it, per base unit since the two can be in different units. Blank for a material never priced, and a blank Change % with one price. One query reads every material's history (`IRawMaterialPriceHistoryRepository.GetAllAsync`).
- **Cost Buffers** (`CostBufferRow`): every priced purchased material's buffered cost against the brand it's costed at, with **Only below target** and **Re-apply Target Buffer** (the Raw Materials notes, "The target buffer").
- **Cost Drivers** (`CostDriverAnalysis`): the purchased materials that make up most of the products in Production's material cost, one sellable unit each, at buffered costs: Raw Material, Category, Products, Cost in Products, Share %, Cumulative % (Pareto), and Largest Share % with the product it's in. Each product is priced with the material at no cost against as it is, as the Price Simulator prices a change (so a material counts however it gets into a product), but only the products the material reaches (`IngredientCostRules.ReachedBy` against `PriceSimulatorReport.MaterialIdsOf`), which keeps the load within the tab's few seconds.
- **Margin Erosion** (`MarginErosionAnalysis`): each product now against its latest version saved by the end of the day picked (a date picker, `SfDatePicker`, 30 days back to start with; `IReportQueries.ReadVersionsAsOfAsync`, over `IProductVersionRepository.GetLatestAsOfAsync`, one `DISTINCT ON` read), the version priced at its costs then (`ProductVersionSnapshot.ToDetail`), listing only those with a current price then and now, with **Only dropped**. The products now are the visit's, as the other tables price them; a new day reads only the versions again, through `LoadLatest`.

## Reading them

- **The reads**: one read a visit (`IReportQueries.ReadAsync`, UseCases, off the UI thread) reads the products with their details, every material, the price history and the BOM lines side by side, and the screen then prices every product once, off the UI thread too (`ReportsState.Tabulate`) (`SavedProductReader.ReadDetailsAsync` and `Build`, on plural repository reads, in about 15 queries however many products there are, every packaging link read once and a composite's products costed bottom-up, once each) for the Product Cost Summary, Cost Drivers and Margin Erosion together. Each read and priced the products for itself until 2026-11-13: 58 round trips a visit, now 22 or 23, and a visit about 10 to 30% quicker (median), in alternating runs on a copy of the dev database. `SavedProductReaderBulkTests` checks that ten products take as many queries as three. `ReportsState` takes its queries (`IReportQueries`, with Margin Erosion's `ReadVersionsAsOfAsync`) and `IRawMaterialEditor`, for Re-apply Target Buffer (since phase 4, ADR 0002).
- **Each table's rows come in one change** (`TableRows.ReplaceWith`), the load's and the Only below target and Only dropped filters' alike, since an `SfGrid` draws all its rows again for each change to its list. Filled a row at a time (`Clear`, then DynamicData's `AddRange`), the tables were drawn some 330 times a visit: a visit took about 9 seconds and held the window for 7 of them, 4 without a break. In one change each, with only the first tab's table built as the page opens, a visit settled in about a second, the window held for 0.6 to 1 of it (2026-11-07, the dev database's 70 products and 129 purchased materials, measured in the window).
- **Rows that show the same as before change nothing**, so a load that finds nothing new, as most visits do, leaves the table drawn with the last visit's rows as it is, and **each grid is in a `SelfDrawn`**, so the page drawing again (a button, a toast, a grid's event) doesn't draw the grids again; each draws itself as its rows change. Cost Buffers' picks are cleared when its rows are replaced, as the grid unpicks them, and as the page opens with a new grid; the same rows keep them. With both (Syncfusion's performance guide, at the user's request), a visit settles in about 0.75 s, the window held for about 0.45 of it; picking a row, a filter, or a first tab of up to 11 rows holds it for nothing measurable, and a first tab of 107 to 129 rows for about 0.1 s. Row virtualization was tried and left out ([maui-app.md](maui-app.md), "How it's built"). The reads, Margin Erosion's included, ran for about half a second in a trace, off the UI thread; Margin Erosion read and priced every product again, some 35 ms of it, until it took the visit's (2026-11-13, above).
- Tests: `ReportsStateTests` (`A_load_fills_each_table_in_one_change`, `A_load_that_finds_nothing_new_leaves_the_tables_alone` and `A_load_that_finds_a_change_replaces_its_table` among them), `ReportAnalysisTests` (`A_visit_reads_the_products_once_for_every_table_and_a_new_day_reads_only_the_versions` among them), `TableRowsTests`, `SelfDrawnTests` and a `BrandPricePersistenceTests` case for `GetAllAsync`. The page's drawing is checked in the app's window (the `run-tinapayanrms` skill).
